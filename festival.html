The Opportunity

Your lineup deserves an official collectible.

Sports has had officially licensed trading cards for decades. Music festivals have apparel. We're changing that — bringing official, artist-approved trading cards into your festival footprint with exclusive on-premise drops that create real lines and a new revenue tool for your sponsors.

The Gap

Festivals don't have official collectibles.

Your lineup is the product. But there's no official collectible built around it — and the vacuum is already being filled by the wrong people.

  • 01

    Uncontrolled Content

    Fan photographers flood social with unlicensed shots of your performers — zero rights clearance, zero quality control attached to your event's name.

  • 02

    Missed Merch Category

    Festival merch is dominated by tees and hoodies. There's no collectible product built around the actual lineup — the artists who define your identity.

  • 03

    Untapped Sponsor Vehicle

    Brand sponsors want activations fans remember beyond Monday. There's no product tying a sponsor's logo to your artists in a format people actually keep.

The Solution

Official cards. Built around your lineup.

01

Official Artist Licensing

Every card is fully licensed. We handle all artist approvals and rights clearance — you never deal with a permissions issue.

02

On-Premise Exclusivity

Your festival is the only place to get these cards. That exclusivity creates urgency, collector demand, and genuine buzz.

03

Sponsor Integration

Cards are a ready-made sponsor vehicle. A brand can co-sign the drop, sponsor the booth, or be printed on the back of every card.

04

Pack-In / Pack-Out

We handle production, booth setup, and staffing. No merch team required on your end. We show up and run it.

From lineup confirmation to cards on the ground.
