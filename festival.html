The Opportunity

Your Lineup Deserves
an Official Collectible.

Sports has had officially licensed trading cards for decades. Music festivals have apparel. We're changing that — bringing official, artist-approved trading cards directly into your festival footprint with exclusive on-premise drops that create real lines, real buzz, and a new revenue tool for your sponsors.

The Gap

Festivals Don't Have
Official Collectibles

Your lineup is the product. But there's no official collectible built around it — and the vacuum is already being filled by the wrong people.

  • 01 Uncontrolled Content

    Fan photographers flood social with unlicensed shots of your performers — with zero rights clearance, zero revenue, and zero quality control attached to your event's name.

  • 02 Missed Merch Category

    Festival merch is dominated by tees and hoodies. There's no collectible product in the market built around the actual lineup — the artists who define your identity.

  • 03 Untapped Sponsor Vehicle

    Brand sponsors want activations fans remember beyond Monday. Tents, samples, and photo booths blur together. There's no product tying a sponsor's logo to your artists in a format people actually keep.

The Solution

Official Cards.
Built Around Your Lineup.

01
Official Artist Licensing

Every card is fully licensed. We handle all artist approvals and rights clearance so you never deal with a permissions issue.

02
On-Premise Exclusivity

Your festival is the only place to get these cards. That exclusivity creates urgency, collector demand, and genuine buzz at the event.

03
Sponsor Integration

Cards are a ready-made sponsor vehicle. A brand can co-sign the drop, sponsor the booth, or be printed right on the back of every card.

04
Pack-In / Pack-Out Logistics

We handle production, booth setup, and staffing. No merch team required on your end. We show up and run it.
