The Problem

Fan-Made Cards
Are Out of Control

Fan-made trading cards are already circulating with your name and face — and you're not seeing a cent or approving a single image.

The White Space

Sports Has Had This
for Decades. Music Hasn't.

Baseball cards. Basketball cards. Football cards. Every major sport has an officially licensed collectible built around its athletes. Music has never had a version of this — until now. Your artists have legacy. Your catalog has value. There's a new format to put both of them in collectors' hands.

The Challenge

Merch Is Saturated.
Streaming Pays Pennies.

The existing options for artist marketing and merch aren't moving the needle. There's a new format sitting right in front of the industry.

  • 01 Merch Is a Commodity

    Tees and hoodies are a race to the bottom. Every artist has the same SKUs. The format doesn't create collector demand — it creates inventory risk.

  • 02 Catalog Activation Is Hard

    How do you market a 30-year-old album in 2026? Reissues and playlists only go so far. There's no physical artifact that brings legacy artists back into cultural conversation.

  • 03 Superfans Have No Physical Anchor

    Streaming is passive. There's no collectible object tying a superfan's identity to the artists they love most. The sports card industry proved this market exists — at massive scale.

The Solution

A New Format.
Built for Superfans.

01
Official Licensed Drops

Officially licensed trading cards for your artists. Artist-approved photography, copy, and stats. Every card is legitimate — no knockoffs, no gray market.

02
Catalog Activation

Perfect for legacy artists, anniversary releases, or tour tie-ins. The card drop IS the marketing moment — press-worthy, social-native, collector-driven.

03
Drop Culture Format

Limited edition. Numbered runs. Scarcity drives demand. Collectors hunt these the same way they hunt sneakers and Supreme drops.

04
Pilot Program

Start with a 3–5 card drop. Low commitment. Proven model. Gauge collector demand before you decide to scale.
