About Headliner Cards

Built by music industry veterans. Designed for the next era of fandom.

The trading card world is our lifelong passion. With a decade of expertise in the music industry, we built a strategy to bridge the gap between artists, fans, and brands — the licensing infrastructure that sports collectibles took for granted, finally rebuilt for music.

How It Works

From handshake to cards in hands.

Same four-step process across every audience path. Whether you're an artist, a festival, a label, or a sponsor — we handle the licensing, you stay in creative control.

01 · Meeting

Discuss the partnership

We respond within 48 hours of your application. Walk through scope, timing, and where the card drop fits in your existing footprint.

02 · Contract

Sign an artist-first agreement

Rights cleared, creative approval rights documented. You stay in control at every stage. We handle the legal paperwork.

03 · Design

Mockups & approvals

Photography, copy, and layouts. Multiple approval rounds. Nothing goes to print without your full sign-off.

04 · Print & Activate

Premium soft-touch laminated cards

Cards delivered, booth packed in, activation staffed. We arrive ready to run it. You get photography back within 72 hours.
